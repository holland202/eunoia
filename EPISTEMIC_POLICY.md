# Epistemic Policy

**Status:** BINDING for all documentation and claims inside this repository

## 1. Rule

Every claim must have an identifiable epistemic status. Unlabelled claims are treated as PROPOSAL by default.

## 2. Status vocabulary

FACT · MEASURED · DERIVED · IMPLEMENTED · HYPOTHESIS · PROPOSAL · SPECULATIVE · UNKNOWN · REFUTED · INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE · NOT_SUPPORTED

## 3. Forbidden inflation

Words such as proves, guarantees, solves, safe, aligned, intelligent, conscious are forbidden unless the exact claim and supporting evidence are stated.

## 4. Implementation vs validity

Implementation conformance ≠ scientific validity.

## 5. Certificates

A certificate validates the specific object and scope for which it was issued. It does not validate a general theory.

## 6. Research streams default status

SWAY: Adopted methodology; predictions largely untested.
DECAY / GROWTH: Frozen specifications.
Companion / topology: Experimental.
Information geometry: RESEARCH / HYPOTHESIS.

## 7. Self-application

Detected defects are recorded. Repair that would contaminate the observation is refused until the observation is sealed. A real failure is required for adoption of a new rule.

## 8. Detection vs authority (founding principle)

Detection of a defect in a methodology creates **evidence**. It does **not** create **authority** to modify that methodology.

Self-application observations remain OPEN until the adoption criterion (real failure under Doors) is met. Automatic rewrite is forbidden.

Founding example: SWAY Amendment 1 → O1 OPEN, P5 OPEN/UNRUN, no Amendment 2.
