# Eunoia Packet

**Status:** PROPOSAL — intake shape for candidate intellectual objects

## Flow

POLYCHORUS (or other generator) → origin: brainstorming → EUNOIA PACKET

Packet fields:
- What is the object?
- What does it predict?
- What mechanism is proposed?
- What alternatives exist?
- What would falsify it?
- What evidence exists?

Then: Theory registry / Hypothesis / SWAY / Evidence / Bayesian model → Epistemic status (never silent → Authority).

## Required fields

object, origin, predictions, mechanism, alternatives, falsifiers, evidence, grain (L1–L5 / L_E), theory_links (optional at intake).

Packets are candidates. Brainstorming origin never implies confirmatory status.
E1–E8 apply immediately.
