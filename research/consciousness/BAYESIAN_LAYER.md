# Bayesian Evidence Layer

**Status:** PROPOSAL

P(C | E, T, A) is epistemic and theory-conditional — not a physical measure of experience.

Every posterior records: value, theory_id, assumptions, evidence_set, model_version.

Model averaging only with exposed theory weights.

Δ_T = max_i P(C|E,T_i) - min_i P(C|E,T_i) → theoretical sensitivity.

assessment_confidence (measurement quality) is distinct from consciousness_credence.

Self-report LR = P(R|C)/P(R|¬C).
