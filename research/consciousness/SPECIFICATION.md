# Eunoia — Consciousness Assessment Framework

**Version:** 0.1 — Foundational Specification
**Status:** Research framework; **not** a validated consciousness detector
**Date:** 30 September 2026

## Binding principle

Evidence about consciousness is not consciousness itself.
P(C | E, T, A) ≠ measured amount of consciousness.

Observation ≠ Interpretation ≠ Theory ≠ Ontological conclusion.

## Explicit non-claims

Intelligence ⇏ consciousness. Agency ⇏ consciousness. Self-report ⇏ consciousness.
Complexity, language ability do not imply consciousness.

## Constructs

I = intelligence/capability | A_g = agency | C = consciousness hypothesis
Four-axis: (I, A_g, C_credence, E_assessment_confidence)

## Five grains + epistemic layer

L1 Behavioral (multiply realizable)
L2 Computational
L3 Intrinsic causal-structural (prefer perturbation; correlation ≠ causation)
L4 Organismic (only if theory requires)
L5 Organism–environment (embodiment ≠ proof)
L_E Evidence/knowledge status

Substrate S is cross-cutting; store assumptions explicitly.

## Theory registry

No default-true theory. Each entry: id, properties, substrate constraints, predictions, falsifiers, prior, provenance.

## Indicators

Operational definition, controls, confounders, evidence_status (default UNVALIDATED). No authority from persuasive names.

## Self-report

LR = P(R|C) / P(R|¬C). Weak when non-conscious paths (imitation, RL, role-play) are likely.

## Bayesian layer

Every posterior records theory, assumptions, evidence_set, model_version.
Δ_T theoretical sensitivity; large Δ_T → THEORY_DEPENDENT (informative).

## Evidence classes

OBSERVED | DERIVED | INFERRED | THEORETICAL | SPECULATIVE | REFUTED | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE | NOT_SUPPORTED
NOT_SUPPORTED ≠ REFUTED.

## Nulls

Prefer: H0 = behavior explainable without proposed internal mechanism.

## Method

Perturbation protocols. Competing predictions. SWAY canopy ≠ elevator. Failed experiments retained. Adversarial scientist role. Rank by expected information gain / cost.

## Outputs

No CONSCIOUS/NOT_CONSCIOUS binary.
ASSESSMENT: theory_conditioned_credence, evidence_strength, theoretical_sensitivity, measurement_quality, confounds, replication, epistemic_status.
No universal C_score.

## Authority firewall

Consciousness evidence ≠ governance authority.
Crossing requires explicit policy (Veritas Gate / Γ).

## Validation ladder

V0 schemas → V1 reference cases → V2 reproducibility → V3 adversarial FP → V4 cross-theory → V5 predictive → V6 external independent.

## No magic metric

Measurement → Construct → Theory → Consciousness hypothesis.
β_k, Fisher F_ij, entropy, Φ-like scores are not consciousness without a validated bridge.

## Progress criteria

Discrimination between mechanisms; adversarial resistance; replication; theoretical robustness; predictive success — not a final metaphysics of mind.

## Principle

Do not ask only whether the system looks conscious.
Ask which observations distinguish the consciousness hypothesis from its strongest non-conscious alternatives.

Measure what can be measured. Expose what must be assumed. Preserve what remains unknown. Update when evidence changes.
Never mistake an inference about consciousness for consciousness itself.
