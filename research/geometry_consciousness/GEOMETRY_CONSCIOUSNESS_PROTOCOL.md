## 4. Rewritten core hypothesis (testable form)

**Not admitted as claim:**

> “The brain's Hilbert space metric tensor deforms from Euclidean space into hyperbolic space.”

**Admitted as hypothesis (requires operationalization):**

> **H1 (representational):** A measurable change in the geometry of an *appropriately defined* neural-state manifold accompanies particular, preregistered conscious-state or cognitive-state transitions, and that geometry yields predictive gain beyond matched baselines.

**Not yet licensed:** consciousness is physically implemented by a hyperbolic Hilbert-space substrate.

```
Does curvature buy predictive information?
```

Same dataset: Euclidean X_E, Hyperbolic X_H, Spherical X_S, Non-geometric nonlinear X_N.
