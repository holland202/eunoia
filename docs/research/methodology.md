# Research Methodology under Eunoia

**Status:** PROPOSAL (integrates SWAY as primary experimental discipline)

## Default discipline: SWAY

SWAY supplies canopy (exploration labelled EXPLORATORY), elevator (registered prediction, anti-vacuity, sealed data), fail-closed guards, DONE criteria.

SWAY is **adopted**, not validated. Its own predictions remain largely untested. See research/sway/.

## Dual instruments

DECAY — What survives removal? (Frozen specification)
GROWTH — What pays when added? (Frozen specification)

SWAY governs confirmatory use of both.

## Minimum experiment record

QUESTION, HYPOTHESIS, NULL, PREDICTION, VARIABLES, CONTROLS, DATASET/ENVIRONMENT, PREREGISTRATION, FORK COUNT, PROCEDURE, RESULT, FAILURE ANALYSIS, REPLICATION STATUS, EPISTEMIC STATUS

Prohibited for confirmatory claims: post-hoc hypothesis rewriting, selective reporting, hidden forks, changing evaluation criteria after seeing results, self-certification, deleting failed experiments.

## Token and resource cost

Where relevant, report baseline and treatment, token counts, latency, thermal/power if measured, quality metric, trials and variance, failure cases. Do not report savings without reporting what was preserved or lost.
