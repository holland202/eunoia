# Philosophy of Eunoia

**Status:** PROPOSAL / conceptual

## Name

εὔνοια (eunoia): good will, beautiful thinking, benevolent disposition.

In this project the term is **not** used to mean “nice AI,” emotional AI, or a friendly chatbot persona.

Technical reading:

> Intelligence that preserves the conditions under which its actions remain justified.

## Central thesis

> Build an architecture in which increasingly capable intelligence can operate without being granted epistemic or operational authority merely because it is intelligent.

Capability and authority are different kinds of thing.
The architecture exists to keep them separate under pressure.

## What “good will” means here

Not sentiment. A system exhibits the relevant disposition when it can maintain the chain:

KNOWN → SUPPORTED → AUTHORIZED → SAFE TO EXECUTE (under policy) → EXECUTED → VERIFIED → RECORDED

And when it refuses to collapse adjacent states:

UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE | NOT_SUPPORTED ≠ REFUTED | PROPOSED ≠ AUTHORIZED | AUTHORIZED ≠ EXECUTED | EXECUTED ≠ VERIFIED

Eunoia is a **synthesis layer**, not a rewrite that erases prior epistemic status.

## Ambition and restraint

Ambition: a coherent architecture for evidence-bounded autonomous intelligence on local hardware.

Restraint: no claim of AGI, sentience, solved alignment, or universal safety.
v0.1 is constitutional substrate, not a finished mind.
