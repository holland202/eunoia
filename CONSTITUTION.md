# Constitution

**Status:** PROPOSAL (foundational constraints for v0.1)
**Authority:** Operator adoption required before these bind any runtime.

## Article I — Epistemic primacy

1. Intelligence does not automatically confer authority.
2. Every operational claim must carry an explicit epistemic status.
3. Status inflation is forbidden.
4. Failed, incomplete, and inconclusive results remain part of the permanent record.

## Article II — Role separation

Model (proposes), Reasoner (transforms), Verifier (evaluates), Authority (permits or refuses), Executor (acts), Attester (records) remain distinguishable. No role may silently inherit another’s authority. Self-certification is forbidden.

## Article III — Fail-closed defaults

Missing evidence → DEFER or REFUSE. Missing authority → REFUSE. Constraint violation → CONTAIN. Failed verification → not promoted. Unknown is preserved as UNKNOWN.

## Article IV — Evidence and provenance

Admissible ≠ sufficient. Implementation conformance ≠ scientific validity. Certificates validate specific objects only.

## Article V — Research discipline

SWAY governs confirmatory experimental claims. DECAY and GROWTH remain research frameworks until experimentally exercised. Exploratory work is labelled EXPLORATORY. Post-hoc rewriting of hypotheses after seeing results is forbidden for confirmatory claims.

## Article VI — Resource honesty

Token, latency, thermal, and energy costs are legitimate experimental variables. Efficiency claims must report what was preserved and sacrificed.

## Article VII — Local-first orientation

Primary design target is local/edge execution.

## Article VIII — Amendment

Constitutional change requires explicit operator decision. “It would be better” is not sufficient without a demonstrated defect or deliberate scope change.
